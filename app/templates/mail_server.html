Before relying on this for real mail: most cloud providers (GCP included) block outbound port 25 by default to fight spam. Receiving mail here works regardless — nothing blocks inbound 25. Sending to external mail servers over raw SMTP (25) will silently fail to deliver until that's unblocked (a support request to your cloud provider) or a smarthost/relay is configured. Port 587 (authenticated submission — what real mail clients use to send) isn't affected by that specific restriction, but test it before depending on it.
