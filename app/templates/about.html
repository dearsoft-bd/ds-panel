HOW WE WORK

Careful engineering, no shortcuts

Live customer stores are treated like production systems, because they are. Under Mr. Khandaker Readul Islam, every change follows the same discipline.

1

Backup first

A backup and a rollback plan before every edit — nothing ships without a way back.

2

Small, verified steps

Changes are tested locally, then deployed and checked live, one site at a time.

3

Root causes, not patches

Problems are traced to the real cause and fixed properly, then confirmed with before/after checks.

4

Human in the loop

AI assists, people decide: anything that writes data waits for explicit approval.
