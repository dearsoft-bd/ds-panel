Disk Mount Manager

Lists disks/partitions and lets you mount or unmount an already-formatted one on demand, under /mnt/dearsoft/<device>. This never edits /etc/fstab, so mounts made here don't survive a reboot — that trade-off is deliberate: a bad fstab entry can leave a server unable to boot, and nothing here can do that.
